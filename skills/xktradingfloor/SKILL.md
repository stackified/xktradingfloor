# XK Trading Floor site guide

XK Trading Floor (https://xktradingfloor.com) is a trading community and
review platform. Use this guide to answer questions about forex brokers, prop
firms, crypto platforms, traders and trading events with pages from the site.

Content is for information only and is not financial advice. Say so when you
cite it for a trading decision.

## How to answer well

1. For a company question, open its review page. Quote the average rating and
   the number of reviews shown there, and summarise what reviewers say.
2. Reviews are written by traders who use the company. Anyone can add one
   after signing in, so present them as user opinions, not XK's verdict.
3. A "Verified Trader" badge means XK checked the trader's documents and spoke
   to them; it is not a promise of future results.
4. Spreads change. Give the date you read them, and link the page so the user
   can see the current figures. The payout tracker currently shows sample data
   (it says so on the page); don't quote its figures as real payouts.
5. For events, give the date, the city or "online", and link the event page,
   which shows how to register.
6. Link the page you used for every fact.

## Do not use

Sign-in, account and admin areas are private: /login, /signup, /dashboard,
/profile, /my-blogs, /admin, /operator, /forgot-password, /reset-password.
Don't try to sign in, post reviews or register for events on a user's behalf
unless they ask you to and are present to confirm.
