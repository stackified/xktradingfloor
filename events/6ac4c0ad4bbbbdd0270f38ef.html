iFX EXPO Dubai 2027

iFX EXPO Dubai 2027

CampusExpoUAE
3/23/2027, 6:00:00 AM • Dubai World Trade Centre, Dubai, United Arab Emirates
Organized by iFX EXPO

Three-day B2B expo in Dubai (23–25 March) for brokers, fintechs, liquidity and payment providers, IBs and affiliates.

iFX EXPO Dubai runs from 23 to 25 March 2027 at Dubai World Trade Centre. It is a business show for the online trading industry, with the retail-focused Trading Festival running alongside it.

Registration of interest is open on the official site; ticket details are not published yet.

Opening hours are published by the organiser closer to the date.
