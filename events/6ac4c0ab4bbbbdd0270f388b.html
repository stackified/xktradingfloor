iFX EXPO Asia 2026

iFX EXPO Asia 2026

CampusExpoAsia
10/8/2026, 2:00:00 AM • Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre (Hall 3FG), Hong Kong
Organized by iFX EXPO

Hong Kong edition of the iFX EXPO business show for the online trading industry: two expo days with exhibitors, talks and the UF AWARDS APAC.

iFX EXPO Asia brings brokers, prop firms, affiliates, IBs, payment and liquidity providers together in Hong Kong. It opens with a welcome party on 7 October (19:30), followed by two expo days on 8 and 9 October, 10:00 to 18:00 local time, with exhibitors, two content stages and the UF AWARDS APAC ceremony.

Industry passes are free to register on the official site.
