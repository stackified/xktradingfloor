Finance Magnates Summit London 2026

Finance Magnates Summit London 2026

CampusExpoUK
11/24/2026, 9:00:00 AM • Old Billingsgate, London, United Kingdom
Organized by Finance Magnates

One-day summit in London for trading, fintech, payments, liquidity and digital-asset firms, with a networking evening the day before.

The Finance Magnates Summit London brings together senior people from brokers, prop firms, fintech, payments, liquidity and digital-asset companies for a day of talks, panels and networking. Some sessions are CPD-accredited.

A Networking Blitz opening event takes place the evening before, on 23 November.

Tickets: free passes for brokers, prop firms, affiliates and IBs; paid passes for banks and service providers. Opening hours are published by the organiser closer to the date.
